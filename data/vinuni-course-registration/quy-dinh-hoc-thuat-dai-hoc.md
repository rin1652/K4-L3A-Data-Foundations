# Academic Regulations for Full-Time Undergraduate Programs

Relevant extract from VinUniversity Academic Regulations V8.1, issued October 30, 2024. The policy applies to full-time undergraduate, exchange and visiting students.

## Article 9. Class Arrangements

The minimum class size for a standard course should be at least 10 students unless the Provost approves otherwise on the Dean’s proposal. A class below the minimum may be cancelled. Students in a cancelled class should be reassigned and must select an alternative course if removing it leaves them below the minimum study load.

## Article 10. Course Registration

At the beginning of each academic year, the Office of Registrar announces the tentative academic calendar, required and elective courses, course descriptions, prerequisites, exam period, and assessment formats.

Students register through the Office of Registrar according to their academic capability, course prerequisites and program requirements. Students are responsible for understanding their curriculum and planning to complete the program within the permitted duration. Registration is accepted only when it complies with University regulations; exceptions require Program Director approval.

## Article 11. Study Load

A normal full-time load is 30 credits over an academic year. Full-time status requires at least 80% of a normal full-time load in a regular semester, equivalent to 12 credits.

- Normal load: at least 14 credits for first-semester students and students whose previous academic standing is good or higher, except the final semester.
- Students with poor academic standing: at least 10 and no more than 14 credits.
- Automatic overload: 18–22 credits, reviewed by the Academic Advisor or Program Director.
- More than 22 credits: requires College Dean or Dean-designee approval.
- Summer semester: up to 9 credits, with no minimum load.

Students must petition for overload if they are first-semester first-year students seeking more than 18 credits, were not in good academic standing and seek more than 18 credits, or seek more than 22 credits in a regular semester. Physical education, support, repeat and non-credit courses count toward overload.

Students must meet course prerequisites and program sequences. A prerequisite exemption requires Program Director approval.

## Article 12. Course Add, Drop, and Withdrawal

Students may add a course no later than the close of the 10th business day of a regular semester or the 5th business day of a mini semester.

A class may be dropped by the published deadline: no later than the close of the 15th business day of a regular semester or the 10th business day of Summer. A change within this period is not recorded in the academic record.

Dropping after the Add/Drop period is a Withdrawal. The transcript receives a “W – Withdrawn” grade, indicating that the student attempted the class but left before completing it for a letter grade. A late Add petition requires approval from the academic advisor or Office of Registrar and is subject to instructor discretion.

Withdrawal must occur before completing more than 30% of the course study time. After that point, withdrawal is not allowed and the student is graded on course performance. The maximum withdrawal allowance is 18 credits over the entire program; after reaching it, students must remain enrolled.
